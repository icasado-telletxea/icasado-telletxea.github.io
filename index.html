About me

Currently I am a Research Associate in machine learning at the Manchester Centre for AI Fundamentals. in Manchester (UK).

Earlier, I was a PhD student under the supervision of Aritz Pérez and Andrés Masegosa at the Basque Center for Applied Mathematics (BCAM), in Bilbao (Basque Country).

My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning and mathematics, with a focus on learning theory, generalization bounds, and Bayesian methods.
