Other research interests

Working-class and labour history

Alongside my work in mathematics and machine learning, I am interested in working-class history, particularly in the Basque Country and Navarre.

My current historical research focuses on Estella-Lizarra during the Franco dictatorship, examining industrial development, labour relations, and the emergence of a new industrial working class.

Industry, discipline, and workers’ experience

I am interested in how factory organisation and workplace discipline shaped everyday life, and how workers responded through individual and collective action.

This research brings together archival documents, workplace regulations, personal collections, and oral testimony.
